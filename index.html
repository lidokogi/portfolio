SOLO PROJECT: UAlbany Shuttle Tracker
Beta Tested
Product & Engineering

Every day, UAlbany students plan their lives around a shuttle they can’t see. The official schedule is a static page: no live location, no real ETA, and no way to know if your shuttle already left or is running late. So I’m building what it should have been, a Google Maps for my school’s shuttle bus. The app shows where the shuttle is right now, plans your trip, and guides you step by step, so you can stop guessing and just go, in turn making campus navigation easier and stress free. I built it with React Native and reverse-engineered the shuttle system’s undocumented API to get live data.

I'm taking it from research to launch myself: I surveyed 100+ students, wrote the PRD, built the app, and ran a beta with about 25 people.

React Native · Expo
Key Features · Core Experience
Live shuttle position on an interactive map, refreshed every 30 seconds
Trip planner that ranks shuttle + walking route combinations by total time
Step-by-step live guidance screen that updates in real time as the trip progresses
Live ETA overlay on full schedules, with weekday/weekend auto-detection
Push notification when the shuttle is about 5 minutes from your stop

Building in real life
Blueprint @ UAlbany
a student org building tech for social good.

I founded and lead Blueprint at SUNY Albany, a student org that builds free, production-grade software for nonprofits. This fall it went from an idea to a 24-person team split into an executive board and two project teams, each with a Product Manager, engineers, designers, and researchers. I'm the president, and I also run one of the teams as its PM.

What I've done so far
Recruited 24 students from about 55 who showed interest, across CS, business, ECE, and nano
Built the club's operating system from scratch: constitution, onboarding, and a two-week sprint cadence
Partnered with the National Women's Law Center as our first nonprofit client
Led discovery with NWLC: interviewed 5 staff, reviewed past user feedback, and turned a manual process into a scoped build.
Grew our social presence on campus while collaborating with other campus organizations to drive engagement
Team A · Building now
Our own chapter website
Project Team A is building the chapter's website, working to give nonprofits and students one place to learn about Blueprint UAlbany and the work we do.
Team B · Building now
National Women's Law Center (NWLC) legal resource directory
NWLC staff currently find help for callers by searching a long document by hand. Project Team B is building a digital, searchable, map-based directory they can update themselves, allowing NWLC users easier, faster access to the help they need.
